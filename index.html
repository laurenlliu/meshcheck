MeshCheck is a website where you can safely check your 3D model to catch potential problems before a model is sent to a 3D printer. Users can upload an STL or 3MF file that they have exported from their 3D modeling software, provide information about what the part is, what it is used for, and how it is expected to function. MeshCheck then analyzes the model’s geometry and printing characteristics to identify issues such as weak areas, unsupported features, non-manifold geometry, difficult-to-print details, and other potential problems. Understanding the purpose of the part allows MeshCheck to provide better feedback.

The goal of MeshCheck is to reduce failed prints and unnecessary wastes of filament and design iterations. Instead of discovering a problem after spending hours waiting for a prototype to be finished printing, MeshCheck gives all of the 3D printing results to you instantly, and also gives you feedback on improvements in your design. We hope to increase the chances that your first print is successful. This allows everyone to spend less time troubleshooting and more time developing and testing their prototypes.

    How MeshCheck works

    1. Drop your STL or 3MF file anywhere on the page, or press the + button. Name your project and say what the part is for.
    2. Pick your printer, nozzle and units in Print settings so the checks match your setup.
    3. Look at your score, then click each item under “What I found”. Problem spots light up on the model and I explain how to fix them.
    4. Try “Best orientation” to cut down on overhangs, download a fixed STL or a report, and ask Tiger AI anything.
