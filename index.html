MeshCheck is a website where you can safely check your 3D model to catch potential problems before a model is sent to a 3D printer. Users can upload an STL, 3MF or OBJ file that they have exported from their 3D modeling software, provide information about what the part is, what it is used for, and how it is expected to function. MeshCheck then analyzes the model’s geometry and printing characteristics to identify issues such as weak areas, unsupported features, non-manifold geometry, difficult-to-print details, and other potential problems. Understanding the purpose of the part allows MeshCheck to provide better feedback.

The goal of MeshCheck is to reduce failed prints and unnecessary wastes of filament and design iterations. Instead of discovering a problem after spending hours waiting for a prototype to be finished printing, MeshCheck gives all of the 3D printing results to you instantly, and also gives you feedback on improvements in your design. We hope to increase the chances that your first print is successful. This allows everyone to spend less time troubleshooting and more time developing and testing their prototypes.

How it works

  1. 1. UploadDrop an STL, 3MF or OBJ file exported from your 3D modeling program.
  2. 2. ReviewSee a score, the exact trouble spots in color, and plain-English advice.
  3. 3. Fix and printRepair many problems with one click, then download the fixed file.

What it looks for

  • Holes and gaps
  • Inside-out faces
  • Overlapping geometry
  • Thin walls
  • Details too small for your nozzle
  • Overhangs and long bridges
  • Sharp points
  • Small holes that print too tight
  • Sealed hollow pockets
  • Parts too close together
  • Fit on your printer
  • Tall, narrow shapes
  • Weak bed contact
  • Time, weight and cost estimates
Your 3D files are checked in your browser and never uploaded. If you sign in, only your scores and settings are saved. Send feedback or report a bug Results are estimates. Always preview your model in your slicer before printing.

        How MeshCheck works

        1. Drop your STL, 3MF or OBJ file anywhere on the page, or press the + button. Name your project and say what the part is for.
        2. Pick your printer, nozzle and units in Print settings so the checks match your setup.
        3. Look at your score, then click each item under “What I found”. Problem spots light up on the model and I explain how to fix them.
        4. Try “Best orientation” to cut down on overhangs, download a fixed STL or a report, and ask Tiger AI anything.
